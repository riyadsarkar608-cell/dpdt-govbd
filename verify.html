Trademark Fee Structure

The following fees are prescribed under the Trademarks Act, 2009 and are payable at the time of filing or renewal with DPDT, Bangladesh.

Application & Filing Fees
Service Description Form No. Fee (BDT)
Application for Registration of a Trademark (per class) New TM-1 ৳ 3,000
Application for Series of Trademarks (per class, per mark) TM-1 ৳ 3,000
Application for Collective Mark or Certification Mark TM-1 ৳ 5,000
Application for Amendment of Registered Trademark TM-26 ৳ 1,500
Application for Registration of Assignment / Transmission TM-24 ৳ 4,000
Certified Copy of Entry in the Register (per copy) TM-31 ৳ 500
Renewal Fees
Service Description Form No. Fee (BDT)
Renewal of Registration (per class) — within period Renewal TM-12 ৳ 4,000
Renewal with Surcharge (late filing within 6 months) TM-12 ৳ 6,000
Restoration of Removed Mark TM-13 ৳ 8,000
Opposition & Legal Fees
Service Description Form No. Fee (BDT)
Notice of Opposition to Registration TM-5 ৳ 3,000
Counter-Statement to Opposition TM-6 ৳ 1,500
Application for Rectification of Register TM-27 ৳ 3,500
Application for Cancellation of Registration TM-28 ৳ 5,000
📌 Important Notice All fees are payable via Treasury Challan (Code: 1-4331-0001-1801) at any scheduled bank. Fees are subject to revision by the Government. For the most current official schedule, please visit dpdt.gov.bd or contact the DPDT office directly. All amounts are in Bangladeshi Taka (BDT ৳).
